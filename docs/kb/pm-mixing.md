# Mixing and beatmatching

## Mix two tracks

1. Load a track on Channel 1 and press **Play**. See [Browsing and loading tracks](pm-browsing-loading.md).
2. Press **Channel 2** and load a second track.
3. Cue it in the headphones: hold the P-switch down and slide the crossfader to the channel to hear (left is channel 1, right is channel 2). Stroke the rim to set phones volume.
4. Match the tempo.
5. Start the second track on the beat and slide the crossfader across.

## Match tempo

### One-press sync

With both channels playing, hold the P-switch down and press **Play** on the channel to adjust. Tempo and phase sync to the other channel.

### By hand

1. Hold the P-switch up.
2. Stroke the rim clockwise or anticlockwise to change tempo. The change and BPM show on screen.
3. Release the P-switch.

A quick stroke along the rim bends the tempo briefly.

### Beat graph

| Display | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White bars drift right | Active track is faster |
| White bars drift left | Active track is slower |
| White bars do not line up with grey bars | Phase is off. Bend the track |

## Crossfader

The crossfader is relative: the indicator follows finger movement, not touch position. Full left is channel 1 only, full right is channel 2 only.

| Setting | Options | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Effect x-fader | Off, Bass, Filter | Folds an effect into the crossfader |
| Phones x-fader | Relative, Normal | How the headphone crossfader shows |

## EQ and gain

1. Touch the middle of the pad.
2. Swipe north, east, south or west.
3. At the rim, stroke around it to change the value, in one continuous movement.

Default layout (P-switch not held):

| Direction | Control |
|---|---|
| North | Treble |
| East | Mid |
| South | Bass |
| West | Gain |

Values reset when a new track loads. Line-out volume: **Settings, Audio, Lineout, Volume**.

## Kill-all

The FX button switches off all EQ and effect changes. A second press restores them. Gain and Key are not affected. Changing any EQ or effect value after kill-all prevents the restore.

## Headphone level

Set phones range (Low or High impedance) and the volume limit under **Settings, Audio, Phones**. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md). Keep headphone volume low. For effects, see [Effects, EQ and the Vocal filter](pm-effects.md).
