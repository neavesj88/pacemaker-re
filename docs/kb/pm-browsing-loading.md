# Browsing and loading tracks

Source: the official User's Manual v2.0 and the firmware's own key handling. See [Controls tour](pm-controls-tour.md) for what "verified" means.

## Open the list and pick a track

1. Press **Channel 1** or **Channel 2** to choose where the track will go. (Verified)
2. **Double-tap the middle of the touchpad** to open the track list. (Verified)
3. **Stroke up or down** on the pad to move the cursor. Tapping the north or south edge steps one row. (Verified)
4. Load the track:
   - **Play** loads it and starts playing.
   - **Cue** loads it ready at the first beat.
   - A **swipe left or right**, or a tap on the west or east edge, loads it too, cued or playing depending on the **Autocue** setting. (Verified)
5. **Forward** and **Rewind** jump to the next or previous initial letter, but only when the list is sorted by title. (Verified)

The **Menu** button enters or leaves the music library.

## Filtering the list

1. In the track list, **hold the P-switch up.** The filter bar shows. (Verified)
2. Slide **left or right** to move between filters, **stroke up or down** to pick a value, and **swipe left or right** to apply it. Release the P-switch to see the filtered list. (Verified)
3. Press **Cue** on several filter entries to combine them, or on an applied one to remove it. Choosing **All** clears the filter. (Verified)

The filters on by default are **Case, Mix, Genre, Artist, Album and BPM**. **Remix, Label, Year, Added, Key, Rating, Type and Bitrate** can be switched on under **Settings, Configure, Track list filters**. (Verified)

## Each channel has its own list, or share one

By default each channel has its **own** track list (**Split**). **Settings, Configure, Track list** changes this to **Linked**, where both channels share one list. (Verified)

## Putting tracks into playlists

A playlist is a **case**: see [Recording mixes and making cases](pm-recording.md). Cases are also made and edited in the app: see [Cases and smart cases](cases.md).

## If a track will not load or loads slowly

A track without a readable beat grid cannot be synced properly: re-analyse it in the app ([BPM, keys and waveforms](bpm-keys-waveforms.md)). Slow loading on a microSD card is covered in [Troubleshooting and FAQ](troubleshooting.md). Audio formats the Pacemaker plays: MP3 up to 320 kbps, M4A (AAC-LC), AIFF, FLAC, WAV, Ogg Vorbis and SND. (Verified)
