# Library gap map

**Gap map** (in the sidebar) shows where the collection is deep and where it is thin.

## Grids

| Grid | Shows |
|---|---|
| BPM × Key | Track count per tempo band and key |
| BPM × Genre | Tempo bands against genres |
| Key × Energy | Needs track energy; otherwise a link offers to analyse it |

Cell colour = track count; thin cells are marked. Tracks with no BPM, key or genre, or a BPM outside the range, are not placed.

## Inspect a cell

1. Click a cell to list its tracks.
2. Press **Show these in Tracks**.

An empty cell shows "That is the gap".

## Settings

**Settings** sets the BPM band width, the BPM range, how many genres are shown and when a cell or row counts as thin. Energy uses the **Energy source** setting ([BPM, keys and waveforms](bpm-keys-waveforms.md)).
