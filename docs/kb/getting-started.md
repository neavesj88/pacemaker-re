# Getting started

Pacemaker Revival edits the library of a Tonium Pacemaker, and it also keeps a library of its own on your computer. You can do nearly everything on the computer and send the result to the Pacemaker later.

## Two kinds of library

- **A library on this computer.** Your tracks, mixes and cases live in a folder here. You do not need the Pacemaker to work on it. Treat it as your master copy.
- **A Pacemaker.** The drive (or the Pacemaker's storage in a card reader) that holds the library the unit plays from. Open it when you want to change what is on the unit itself.

The library picker at the top of the window switches between them. The sidebar has the same entries for both, except the tools that only make sense for one (Free space is for a Pacemaker, and the sync tools need a library on this computer).

## The Welcome screen

When no library is open, the Welcome screen offers:

- **Start a library on this computer.** It makes a folder called Pacemaker Revival in your Music folder (you can choose another place first). Nothing is made until you press the button.
- **Copy my Pacemaker's library to this computer.** Shown when a Pacemaker is connected. It makes the library here, then shows what would be copied before anything is.
- **Open a Pacemaker.** Plug the Pacemaker in over USB (it appears as a drive) and press Rescan. You can also open any folder that contains a `.Pacemaker` library.

If your settings say the library is on a drive that is not connected, the Welcome screen says so. Connect the drive and press Rescan. It does not make a new empty library behind your back.

## Adding tracks

Press **Add tracks...** at the top, or drop files or folders anywhere in the window. Each file is analysed, copied into the library and added with its beat grid. You can also bring in lists from other software: see [Import and export](import-export.md).

## What happens next

- **Analysis.** Adding a track works out its BPM and beat grid straight away. Waveforms, keys, energy and sections carry on in the background, so you can keep working. See [Background analysis](background-analysis.md).
- **Tracks view.** Everything you add shows up in [the Tracks view](tracks-view.md), where you search, sort, tag and rate.
- **Mixes and cases.** Build [cases](cases.md) (playlists) and [mixes](mixes-building.md) from the tracks.
- **The Pacemaker.** When you are ready, use [Sync](sync.md) to copy tracks, mixes and cases to the unit.

## Good habits

- Back up a Pacemaker's library before changing anything big: see [The Device page](device-page.md).
- Every tool that writes shows you what it will change first. Read that list.
- Press F1 at any time to open this help. See [Using the Help](using-help.md).
