# Recording mixes and making cases

## Record a mix

Mix save records a mix in Mix mode so it can be played back, edited and exported on the computer.

1. Switch to Mix mode.
2. Press the settings button and choose **Mix save**.
3. Swipe right to switch it **On**. Recording starts.
4. Mix.
5. Open **Mix save** again and swipe right to switch it **Off**.
6. Choose **Save**, **Discard** or **Cancel**.

**Mix save** is off after every start. It belongs to Mix mode.

## Pause, lock or split a recording

**Halt** (settings menu) interrupts the current mix without leaving Mix mode.

1. Press the settings button and choose **Halt**.
2. Pick an option and swipe right.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Continue | Resumes mixing |
| Save | Saves the mix recorded so far |
| Lock | Locks the Pacemaker against input so you can take a break |
| New | Saves the current mix and starts a new recording |

To unlock, choose **Unlock** in the settings menu.

## Use recorded mixes

1. Connect the Pacemaker and open **Sync**, or the two-pane **Libraries** view: see [Sync](sync.md) and [Libraries](libraries.md).
2. Copy the mixes to the computer library.
3. Open one on the Mixes page to edit it: see [Building a mix](mixes-building.md). To make an audio file, see [Mix tools and audio export](mixes-tools-export.md).

Mixes sent from the computer to the Pacemaker appear as track lists. Each track's start becomes a cue point on that track. Effect curves and automation are not played.

## Create a case

A case is the Pacemaker's playlist.

1. Open the track list by double-tapping the centre of the touchpad.
2. Select a track.
3. Press the settings button. The track list menu opens.
4. Choose **Add to case** and swipe right.
5. Choose **Create case** and swipe right. A case named "New Case" plus a counter is made.
6. In the **Add to case** menu choose the new case and swipe right. The track is added.
7. Press the settings button to leave the menu at any point.

## Add a track to an existing case

1. Open the track list and select a track.
2. Press the settings button, choose **Add to case** and swipe right.
3. Choose **My cases** and swipe right.
4. Choose the case and swipe right.

Adding the same track twice creates a second row.

## Add every listed track to a case

In the track list menu choose **Add all to case** instead of **Add to case**. Every track in the currently filtered list is added. Filter first (by genre, BPM and so on) to add only a subset: see [Browsing and loading tracks](pm-browsing-loading.md).

## Remove a track from a case

1. Open the track list.
2. Hold the P-switch up to show the track list filters and go to **Case**.
3. Choose the case and release the P-switch. Only its tracks are listed.
4. Select the track.
5. Press the settings button, choose **Remove** and swipe right.
6. In the **Remove from case** menu choose **Remove** and swipe right to confirm.

The track stays in the library. Only its place in the case is removed.
