# Library gap map

**Gap map** (in the sidebar) shows where your collection is deep and where it is thin. It is worked out from the tracks already loaded. Nothing is read from or written to a Pacemaker.

## The grids

Three grids, chosen by the tabs:

- **BPM × Key**: how many tracks you have in each tempo band and key.
- **BPM × Genre**: tempo bands against genres.
- **Key × Energy**: appears once tracks have an energy (your rating or the estimate). If it has not, a link offers to analyse energy.

The colour of a cell shows how many tracks it holds (the legend runs from few to many) and **thin** cells are marked. The line above the grid says how many tracks were placed and why others were left out (no BPM because they still need analysis, no key, no genre, a BPM outside the range shown).

## Using it

- Click a cell to list its tracks (the first ones by BPM) and press **Show these in Tracks** to see them all. An empty cell says "That is the gap".
- The summary of thin spots says which rows and cells are thin, so you know what to look for next.
- **Settings** sets the width of a BPM band, the BPM range shown, how many genres are shown and when a cell or row counts as thin. The energy used is the **Energy source** setting: see [BPM, keys and waveforms](bpm-keys-waveforms.md).
