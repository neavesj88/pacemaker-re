# Power and charging

## Switch on

Press the **settings button**.

## Switch off

Choose **Shutdown** in the settings menu, or hold the settings button for more than four seconds.

## Charge

- USB: switch the Pacemaker off and connect the cable to a computer. The Play button blinks while charging.
- Adapter: connect it to the power jack. Fastest.

To give the computer access to the drive while charging over USB, press the **settings button**. See [Connecting to a computer](pm-computer-connection.md).

A blinking power symbol means the battery needs charging.

## Battery life

| Mode | Runtime |
|---|---|
| Mix mode | About 5 hours |
| Play mode | About 15 hours |

Worn batteries last far less. See [Replacing the battery](battery-replacement.md).

## Save power

Lower the brightness and set a shorter timeout and standby time in the settings menu, Power folder. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).
