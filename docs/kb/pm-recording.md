# Recording mixes and making cases

Source: the official User's Manual v2.0 and the firmware's settings. See [Controls tour](pm-controls-tour.md) for what "verified" means.

## Recording a mix

1. Be in **Mix mode** (not Play mode).
2. Open **Settings** and set **Mix save** to **On**. Recording starts. (Verified)
3. Mix as usual.
4. Set **Mix save** to **Off**. Choose **Save**, **Discard** or **Cancel**. (Verified)

**Mix save is switched off every time the player starts**, so a recording never continues across a restart. **Halt** in the settings offers **Continue, Save, Lock and New** (New starts a new recording). (Verified)

Saved mixes transfer to the Editor (this app, or Tonium's original) for editing. In the app, import them from the Mixes page: see [Building a mix](mixes-building.md).

**Mixes sent from the Editor to the Pacemaker appear there as track lists only**, with each track's start as a cue point. The Pacemaker cannot reproduce automation made in an Editor mix (effect curves and so on). (Verified)

## Cases (playlists)

A **case** is the Pacemaker's playlist.

**Add a track:** in the track list, select the track, press the **settings button** to open the track list menu, and choose **Add to case**, then **Create case** or **My cases**, and swipe right. **Add all to case** adds every track in the filtered list. (Verified)

**Remove a track:** hold the P-switch up in the track list and filter by **Case**, select the case, select the track, press the settings button, choose **Remove** and confirm. (Verified)

Notes:

- A new case is named "New Case" with a counter. (Verified)
- Adding a track twice makes a **duplicate row**: the Pacemaker does not check. (Verified from the firmware)
- Making and editing cases is far easier in the app: see [Cases and smart cases](cases.md). A smart case can keep a case on the Pacemaker up to date.

## A favourite button

The notes describe a one-button "Favourite" idea (P-switch down plus Channel 1 adds the highlighted track to a case called Favourites). **It does not exist.** No such firmware has been built, and that key combination does nothing today. The heart in the app puts a track in a case called Favourites instead: [The Tracks view](tracks-view.md).
