# Cues, loops and reverse

All of these work on the active channel in Mix mode. The loop and reverse controls need the P-switch held up: see [Controls tour](pm-controls-tour.md).

## Set a cue point

A cue point is a saved position you can jump back to. Each track has one user cue point, and loading another track on the channel clears it. Cue points set in the [beat grid editor](grid-editor.md) or copied from a computer library stay on the track until you change them.

1. With a track playing, press **Play**. The track DJ-pauses: it stutters at the current spot, **Play** and **Cue** blink and the DJ-pause symbol shows at the top centre of the display.
2. Move the position. Either hold **Forward** or **Rewind** to run through the track, or stroke the touchpad rim for fine steps. The track keeps stuttering at the new spot.
3. Press **Cue**. The track is cued there, **Cue** lights and the cue symbol shows.

With **Vinyl mode** on, the pause scrubs like a record instead of stuttering: see [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md#settings-mix-control).

> **Autocue** (on by default) cues every loaded track at its first beat. Turn it off under Settings, Mix control.

## Use the cue point

1. Press **Cue** while playing to jump back to the cue point.
2. Hold **Cue** to audition: the track plays from the cue point while you hold it and returns to it when you let go.
3. To keep playing, press **Play** before you release **Cue**.

## Make a loop

A loop repeats the part of a track between an in point and an end point. Each track keeps one loop, and setting a new one overwrites it. A loop stays saved and can be re-entered after other tracks have been loaded.

1. Hold the P-switch up.
2. Press **Rewind** at the in point. **Rewind** lights, **Forward** blinks and the loop symbol blinks.
3. Press **Forward** at the end point.
4. Release the P-switch. The track now loops, **Cue**, **Forward** and **Rewind** blink and the loop symbol is steady.

Releasing the P-switch, reversing or changing tempo between steps 2 and 3 cancels the loop.

## Exit and re-enter a loop

1. Hold the P-switch up and press **Cue** to leave the loop. Playback continues normally from the end point and **Cue** blinks.
2. Hold the P-switch up and press **Cue** again to return to the saved loop.

Pressing **Cue**, fast-forwarding or rewinding inside a loop also exits it.

## Move the loop points

Works only while the loop is running.

| To move | Steps |
|---|---|
| In point | Hold the P-switch up and press **Rewind** (**Rewind** blinks, **Forward** lights). Stroke the rim clockwise or anti-clockwise to move the point forward or backward (which direction is which is not confirmed). Release the P-switch. |
| End point | Hold the P-switch up and press **Forward** (**Forward** blinks, **Rewind** lights). Stroke the rim the same way. Release the P-switch. |

**Loop travel** (Settings, Mix control, on by default) decides whether moving one point pushes the other along: see [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md#settings-mix-control).

## Halve and double a loop

Works only while the loop is running. Splitting shortens the loop by half per tap. Doubling lengthens a split loop by tapping the opposite way, and a split loop can only be doubled back up to its original length. Hold the P-switch up for all four.

| Goal | Hold | Tap | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Halve toward the in point | **Rewind** | **Forward**, once per halving | The end point moves halfway back toward the in point |
| 2. Double a loop halved in 1 | **Forward** | **Rewind**, once per doubling | The end point moves back out |
| 3. Halve toward the end point | **Forward** | **Rewind**, once per halving | The in point moves halfway forward toward the end point |
| 4. Double a loop halved in 3 | **Rewind** | **Forward**, once per doubling | The in point moves back out |

1. Hold the P-switch up and keep it up for the whole gesture.
2. Hold the button from the Hold column.
3. Tap the button from the Tap column as many times as needed.
4. Release the held button or the P-switch.

Procedures 1 and 4 use the same buttons, as do 2 and 3. Doubling applies only to a loop that has been halved.

## Play in reverse

1. Hold the P-switch up and press **Play**. The track plays backwards, **Play** blinks and the reverse symbol shows at the top centre.
2. Hold the P-switch up and press **Play** again to return to forward playback. **Play** lights and the play symbol shows.

## Loop length settings

Under Settings, Mix control the menu also lists **Fixed loop length** (Off, 1/64 to 4, default Off), **Snap loops** (On, Off, default On) and **Beat jump length** (1/16 to 8, default 1/2). The exact audio effect of these three is not confirmed. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md#settings-mix-control).
