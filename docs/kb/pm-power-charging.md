# Power and charging

Source: the official User's Manual v2.0, unless marked. See [Controls tour](pm-controls-tour.md) for what "verified" means.

## Switching on and off

- **On:** press the **settings button**. (Verified)
- **Off:** choose **Shutdown** in the settings menu, or **hold the settings button for more than four seconds**. (Verified)
- Shutting down properly matters: the Pacemaker saves its settings when it shuts down, and creates the settings file the first time it does.

## Charging

- Switch the Pacemaker **off**, then connect the USB cable to a computer. The screen shows a charging screen, then goes blank, and the play button blinks. (Verified)
- Or connect the **power adapter** to the power jack. The adapter is the fastest. Charging speed over USB depends on the port. (Verified)
- While charging over USB, press the **settings button** to turn on the data connection if you want the computer to see the drive: see [Connecting to a computer](pm-computer-connection.md). (Verified)
- A **blinking power symbol** means the battery needs charging. (Verified)
- The Pacemaker can run from the adapter with the battery flat or even absent (reported by users). Useful for a long set or a test. (Likely)

## How long it lasts

- About **five hours in Mix mode** and about **fifteen in Play mode**, from the 2009 manual. These are not re-measured on an old battery. Worn cells last far less (reports of 30 to 45 minutes). (Verified as the manual's figures)
- **Play mode** is a simpler player (Repeat all, one or off, and Shuffle) that uses less power. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).
- The screen's brightness, timeout and standby time are in the settings menu (Power folder). Lower brightness and a shorter timeout save power.

If the battery is worn, see [Replacing the battery](battery-replacement.md).
