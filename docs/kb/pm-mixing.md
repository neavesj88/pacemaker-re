# Mixing and beatmatching

## Mix two tracks

1. Load a track on Channel 1 and press **Play**. See [Browsing and loading tracks](pm-browsing-loading.md).
2. Press **Channel 2** and load a second track.
3. Cue it in the headphones: [monitor the channel](pm-mixing.md#monitor-in-the-headphones).
4. [Match the tempo](pm-mixing.md#match-tempo).
5. Start the second track on the beat and slide the crossfader across.

To set where a track starts, see [Cues, loops and reverse](pm-cues-loops.md).

## Blend with the crossfader

1. Slide a finger along the crossfader strip. The indicator follows your movement.
2. Slide fully left for channel 1 only, fully right for channel 2 only, or stop between them for a blend.

The crossfader works whichever channel is active. It is relative: put your finger anywhere on the strip and the indicator moves by the distance you slide, not to where you touch.

| Setting | Options | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Effect x-fader | Off, Bass, Filter | Folds an effect into the crossfader |
| Phones x-fader | Relative, Normal | How the headphone crossfader shows |
| Lineout warning | On, Off | Warns when you try to disturb the line-out channel |

Set them under **Settings, Mix control**. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).

## Monitor in the headphones

1. Hold the P-switch down.
2. Slide the crossfader fully left to hear channel 1, fully right to hear channel 2, or between them for a blend.
3. Stroke the touchpad rim clockwise or anticlockwise to raise or lower the phones volume.
4. Release the P-switch.

The line-out volume is under **Settings, Audio, Lineout, Volume**.

## Match tempo

### One-press sync

With both channels playing, hold the P-switch down and press **Play** on the channel to adjust. Tempo and phase sync to the other channel. This works at any time while two tracks play.

### By hand

1. Hold the P-switch up.
2. Stroke the rim clockwise to raise the tempo, anticlockwise to lower it. The tempo change and the new BPM show on screen.
3. Release the P-switch.

### Bend

A quick stroke along the rim speeds up or slows down the tempo for a moment. Bend must be switched on in the settings menu first; it is optional. The BPM of the active channel shows at the bottom of the screen while you change the tempo.

### Read the beat graph

With both channels playing, the beat graph shows tempo and phase of the active channel (white bars) against the other channel (grey bars).

| Display | Meaning | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| White bars drift right | Active track is faster | Lower its tempo until the bars stop |
| White bars drift left | Active track is slower | Raise its tempo until the bars stop |
| White bars do not line up with grey bars | Phase is off | Bend the track until they align |

When the bars stop drifting the BPMs match. When they line up the beats coincide. Listen for the downbeat when you are unsure. The graph can be switched off with **Beat graph** under Mix control.

## Adjust EQ and gain

1. Touch the middle of the pad.
2. Swipe north, east, south or west.
3. At the rim, stroke around it to change the value, in one continuous movement. Do not lift your finger between steps.

Default layout (P-switch not held):

| Direction | Control |
|---|---|
| North | Treble |
| East | Mid |
| South | Bass |
| West | Gain |

Values apply to the current track only and reset when a new track loads. Change them one after another to stack them. The layout can be changed: see [Effects, EQ and the Vocal filter](pm-effects.md#change-the-layout).

## Kill-all

1. Press **Kill-all (FX)**. All EQ, colour and beat effect changes switch off.
2. Press it again to restore them.

Gain and Key are not affected. Changing any EQ or effect value after kill-all prevents the restore.

## Headphone level

Set phones range (Low or High impedance), the volume limit and phones EQ under **Settings, Audio, Phones**. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).

> Keep headphone volume low. Loud headphones can damage hearing.

For effects, see [Effects, EQ and the Vocal filter](pm-effects.md).
