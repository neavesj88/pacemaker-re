# The Settings page

**Settings** (in the sidebar, under Tools) gathers the settings of every part of the app in one place. Nothing here is a second copy of a control: each entry shows the same controls you find where the feature is used, so a value changed here is changed there too.

Settings are kept on this computer, not on the Pacemaker. The Pacemaker's own settings (backlight, effects layout and so on) are on the Device page: see [The Device page](device-page.md) and [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).

## Searching

The search box matches the title, the one-line help, the keywords and the area name of every entry. All the words you type must match. When you type something, a **Help** group at the bottom also lists the help articles that match, so a question such as "half tempo" finds both the setting and the article that explains it. Click a result to open the article.

## Interface size

The slider sets the size of everything in the window, from a small laptop to a very large monitor. Ctrl and + make it bigger, Ctrl and - smaller and Ctrl and 0 put it back to the default. If things no longer fit, pick a smaller size.

## Playhead smoothness

How often the line that moves along the waveform is redrawn while a track plays: up to 60 a second (the default), up to 30, or at every refresh of the screen. On a 144 Hz screen the default uses roughly a third to half less processor time than redrawing every refresh and looks the same; 30 is gentler on an older computer or a laptop on battery. The sound and the position the player keeps are not affected.

## Waveform colours

Two choices: **Matrix green, calm** (the default, the app's own green in three quiet steps) and **Neon** (pink, lilac and bright green). They apply to the player, the track pictures and the grid editor. The clips of a mix keep their own colours so you can tell them apart. See [BPM, keys and waveforms](bpm-keys-waveforms.md).
