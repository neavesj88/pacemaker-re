# Power and charging

## Switch on

Press the **settings button**. Revival firmware shows its own boot logo instead of the original one.

## Switch off

- Press the settings button and choose **Shutdown** in the menu, or
- hold the settings button for more than four seconds.

## Charge over USB

1. Switch the Pacemaker off.
2. Connect the USB cable to the Pacemaker.
3. Connect the other end to a computer.
4. A charging screen appears. It later goes dark, and the blinking Play button shows the unit is still charging.

To give the computer access to the drive while charging, press the **settings button**. See [Connecting to a computer](pm-computer-connection.md).

Charging speed depends on the USB port, so a weak port charges slowly.

## Charge with the power adapter

The adapter is sold separately and is the fastest way.

1. Plug the adapter lead into the **Power** jack on the unit.
2. Put the adapter in a wall outlet.

> Use only a Pacemaker power adapter, on an outlet of the voltage printed on it. Do not open the unit or pull out the Li-ion battery.

## Read the power status

| Power symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Steady | Current charge level, shown when no power source is connected |
| Blinking | The battery needs charging |

The symbol is element 9 of [the play screen](pm-controls-tour.md#the-play-screen). Settings, About, Status shows the battery in percent.

## Battery life

| Mode | Runtime |
|---|---|
| Mix mode | About 5 hours |
| Play mode | About 15 hours (up to three times Mix mode) |

Switch between them in the settings menu. A unit left unused for a long time may need a charge before it starts. Worn batteries last far less. See [Replacing the battery](battery-replacement.md).

## Save power

Lower the brightness and set a shorter timeout and standby time in the settings menu, Power folder. Use Play mode when you do not need two channels. See [The settings menu](pm-settings-menu.md).
